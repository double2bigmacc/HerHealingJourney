Social Loneliness

Social media & online pressure

Social loneliness is the feeling of being alone or not having enough meaningful connections with other people. A person may feel socially lonely when they do not have close friends, feel left out, or find it difficult to connect with others. It can make someone feel sad, isolated, or unhappy.

Academic and finacial stress

Social loneliness can be overcome by making an effort to connect with other people and build meaningful relationships. Students can join clubs, sports teams, or social activities where they can meet people with similar interests. Talking to friends, family members, teachers, or classmates can also help reduce feelings of loneliness. Being friendly, starting conversations, and spending more time with others can help build stronger connections. It is also important to be patient because developing close friendships takes time.
