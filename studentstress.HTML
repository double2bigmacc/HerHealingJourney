Student Stress

Social media & online pressure

Student stress is the pressure and worry that students experience because of schoolwork, exams, assignments, deadlines, and expectations. Too much stress can make students feel tired, anxious, or overwhelmed and can affect their sleep and concentration. Students can manage stress by planning their time, taking regular breaks, getting enough sleep, exercising, and talking to someone they trust.

Academic and finacial stress

you can overcome by managing time wisely, taking regular breaks, and getting enough sleep. Students should make a study plan and complete their work on time to avoid feeling overwhelmed. Exercise, healthy food, and relaxing activities can also help reduce stress. It is important for students to talk to their friends, family, teachers, or counsellors when they feel stressed. By staying organised and taking care of their mental and physical health, students can manage stress and feel more confident.
