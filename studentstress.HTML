Student Stress

what student sress is

Student stress is the pressure and worry that students experience because of schoolwork, exams, assignments, deadlines, and expectations.

how to overcome

you can overcome by managing time wisely, taking regular breaks, and getting enough sleep. Students should make a study plan and complete their work on time to avoid feeling overwhelmed.
