HomeStress

Social media & online pressure

If you mean home stress, it is the stress or worry a person experiences because of problems or difficulties at home. This could include family arguments, financial problems, too many responsibilities, lack of privacy, or pressure from family members. Home stress can make someone feel worried, sad, angry, or tired and may affect their schoolwork and everyday life.

Academic and finacial stress

Home stress can be overcome by talking openly with family members and sharing your feelings with someone you trust. It can also help to stay organised, manage responsibilities, and take time to relax and do enjoyable activities. Students should try to get enough sleep, exercise, and focus on things they can control. If problems at home become too difficult to handle, talking to a teacher, counsellor, or another trusted adult can provide support and guidance.
